FAQ

Everything you might wonder about Selfie Studio.

Is Selfie Studio really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no watermark and no limits. If you'd like to help it grow, there's a "Support the project" link at the bottom of the home page.

How does it work?

Tap step inside, allow the camera, choose stickers and a look, then press take photos (or the space bar). There's a 3-second countdown before each of the four photos. Your strip is then ready to share, save or print.

Do you keep my photos?

No. Everything happens in your browser, on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to us. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I share my strip on WhatsApp or AirDrop?

After the photos, press share. On iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac (Safari) and Windows, this opens your device's share sheet, where you'll find WhatsApp, AirDrop, Messages, Instagram and your other apps. If your browser can't share files, you'll get a small menu instead. The WhatsApp button there copies the strip and opens WhatsApp, so you can paste it into a chat.

How do I install Selfie Studio on my computer (for automatic printing)?

The website can take, share and save photos. To print automatically to a Wi-Fi printer, Selfie Studio also needs to run on a computer on the same Wi-Fi. It's free and takes about two minutes.

Mac: open Terminal (⌘ Space → type “Terminal” → Return), paste curl -fsSL https://selfiebooth.pages.dev/install.sh | bash and press Return.

Windows: open PowerShell from the Start menu, paste irm https://selfiebooth.pages.dev/install.ps1 | iex and press Enter.

It installs into a “Selfie Studio” folder in your home folder, downloads what it needs (no admin password), adds a Selfie Studio shortcut to your Desktop and opens the booth at localhost:8080. Next time, double-click the shortcut. Run the same line again any time to update.

How does automatic printing work?

Run the Selfie Studio app on a computer on the same Wi-Fi as your photo printer: double-click Start Selfie Studio, or run npm start. It finds AirPrint, Mopria and other IPP printers on the network by itself. As soon as a printer is found, every new strip is sent to it on 4×6 paper. If you took photos before the printer was on, the waiting strip prints the moment the printer appears.

How do I connect a Canon SELPHY CP1300 (or CP1500)?

1. On the SELPHY, connect it to your Wi-Fi: Wi-Fi settings → Connection method → Via Wi-Fi network (not “Direct connection”). Use the same Wi-Fi as your computer. Menu names can differ slightly by firmware. 2. Load postcard paper and an ink cassette, and leave the SELPHY on its home screen. 3. On the computer, double-click Start Selfie Studio (Mac or Windows) from the Selfie Studio folder. If it asks for Node.js, install the free LTS version once. 4. The booth opens in your browser and finds the SELPHY by itself. Open the printer button at the top and press test print. Strips are laid out on the SELPHY's 100×148 mm postcard paper, borderless, two per sheet.

Selfie Studio looks for printers with Bonjour, and if your router blocks that, it checks every address on your Wi-Fi. If the SELPHY still doesn't appear, look up its IP address on the printer (Wi-Fi settings → Confirm settings), type it into the printer settings (for example 192.168.100.102), press check, then use this printer. On a Mac, make sure Terminal is allowed under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. The online version of Selfie Studio (the website) can't reach printers on your home network by itself. On a Mac it uses the normal print dialog instead: choose the SELPHY and Postcard paper.

Can I use a Bluetooth printer?

Yes. Pair the printer with the computer running Selfie Studio and add it as a printer in that computer's settings (on a Mac: System Settings → Printers & Scanners). Selfie Studio then detects it and prints to it automatically, the same as a Wi-Fi printer. Web browsers can't talk to Bluetooth printers by themselves, so this pairing step is needed. On a phone or tablet, use print or share to send the strip to your printer's app.

What paper do I need, and how do I cut it?

Standard 4×6 inch (10×15 cm) photo paper. Each sheet holds two identical 2×6 strips side by side. Cut down the middle along the long edge, where the small marks are, to get two strips.

Does it work on iPhone and iPad?

Yes, in Safari on iOS and iPadOS 15 or newer. For an event, add it to your home screen (Share → Add to Home Screen) and use Guided Access to keep guests in the booth.

Why can't it see my camera?

Allow camera access when your browser asks. If you said no by mistake, tap the camera or lock icon in the address bar and allow it. The camera also needs a secure (https) page, and it can't be in use by another app at the same time.

Can I use a professional camera?

Yes. Connect a DSLR or mirrorless camera through an HDMI capture card, or over USB-C in webcam mode, then pick it in the camera tab.

What are the stickers, stamps and looks?

Tap any of the 16 stickers to add it to your photos. Stickers stay around the edges, away from faces. In the strip tab you can tilt photos like snapshots, turn them into vintage postage stamps (portrait or square), pick a black, vintage or white background and add a caption. The look tab has colour, black-and-white and bright white filters.
