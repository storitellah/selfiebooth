Printer

Install Selfie Studio on your computer (2 minutes, free)

Mac: open Terminal (press ⌘ Space, type “Terminal”, press Return), paste this line and press Return:

curl -fsSL https://selfiebooth.pages.dev/install.sh | bash

Windows: open PowerShell (Start menu → type “PowerShell”), paste this line and press Enter:

irm https://selfiebooth.pages.dev/install.ps1 | iex

It installs into a “Selfie Studio” folder, adds a Selfie Studio shortcut to your Desktop and opens the booth with your Wi-Fi printer connected. Next time, just double-click the shortcut. When macOS asks to “find devices on your local network”, choose Allow.

    Setting up a Canon SELPHY (CP1300 / CP1500)

    1. Load postcard paper and an ink cassette. 2. On the SELPHY, open Wi-Fi settings → Connection method → Via Wi-Fi network and join the same Wi-Fi as this computer (not “Direct connection”). 3. Keep the SELPHY on the home screen. It appears above within a few seconds; press test print. If it doesn't appear, find its IP address under Wi-Fi settings → Confirm settings, type it above (for example 192.168.100.102) and press check.

    On a Mac: the first time, macOS asks whether Terminal may “find devices on your local network”. Choose Allow. If you missed it, turn it on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network, then restart Selfie Studio.

    Bluetooth or USB printers: connect the printer to this computer and add it in the system's printer settings. It then appears in the list above.
