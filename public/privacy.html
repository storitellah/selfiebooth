Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The short version: your photos stay on your device. Selfie Studio has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and Storitellah never receives or stores your pictures.

Who we are

Selfie Studio is a free photo booth made by Storitellah (storitellah.com). If you have a question about privacy, message us on Instagram at @storitellah or through our website.

Your camera

Selfie Studio asks for camera access only when you step inside the studio. The camera feed is shown and processed in your browser, on your device. It is not recorded, streamed or sent anywhere. The camera switches off when you leave the studio, and you can withdraw permission at any time in your browser settings.

Your photos

  • Photos, filters, stickers and strips are created in your browser, on your device.
  • We don't upload your photos to Storitellah or to any cloud service, and we can't see them.
  • Your photos are kept in your browser's memory only while the page is open. Starting a new strip or closing the page clears them.
  • Photos only leave your device when you choose to share, save or print them (see below).

Sharing, saving and printing

  • Share hands the photo to your device's share sheet (for example WhatsApp, AirDrop or Messages). What happens next is up to you and those apps, under their own privacy policies.
  • Save downloads the image to your device.
  • Print: if Selfie Studio is running on a computer on your own Wi-Fi network, the 4×6 print is sent from your browser to that computer and then to your printer over Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or USB. It stays on your local network. Otherwise, printing uses your device's own print or share sheet.

What is stored on your device

Selfie Studio remembers a few preferences in your browser's local storage, such as your chosen look, background, stickers, camera and print settings, so they're ready next time. No photos, captions or personal details are stored. You can clear this at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Information we don't collect

We don't ask for your name, email or phone number. We don't use cookies for tracking, analytics, advertising or social media pixels.

Services that help run the site

  • Hosting: the website is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the page and protect it from abuse. See the Cloudflare privacy policy.
  • Fonts: the handwritten fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it delivers them. See the Google privacy policy.
  • Support links: Patreon, Ko-fi, M-Pesa and Instagram are only contacted if you choose to follow those links. Their own privacy policies apply.

Children

Selfie Studio is family-friendly and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. We recommend that a parent or guardian helps younger children when they share or print photos.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
